RFTM
Ensure you have:
- 80% Lugol solution
	- 50% lugol solution is too low
- Sterilization station
- Empty beaker to dump the RFTP
	- for every 100 mL, add 5 mL of 100% bleach
To process/stain the samples:
- Get two trays
	- Tray one:
		- Put paper towel down and place slides
	- Tray Two 
		- put paper towel down and have lid to cover tissued slides to prevent degradation 
- Best to do four oysters at a time
- Bring bucket with water and soap to soak tubes
- Mantle and rectum are the tissues sampled
	- Two slides per oyster
- Place a drop of lugol on the slide first
- Grab tube and tip it partially back and forth to dislodge the tissues with forced and grab tissues and place on slide on tray one
	- Dump rest of RFTM on beaker
	- Can use the same forceps
- Mangle the tissue to spread solution and continue to thin out tissue until tissue turns golden in color
- Place cover slip and dump remaining lugol onto the tray (hold the slide and cover slip together and and put a little lugol on it again 
- Place slide under cover on tray two
- Sterilize forceps in-between tissues


How to identify perkinsus under the microscope:
- They tend to be in clusters
- Thin borders  
- Very circular and stains black
	- May have a clear center with thin borders
- When you have finer focus, center does not change

Eggs tend to:
- Stain brown 
- Have a thicker band
- More oval shape
- Have a peak at the top
