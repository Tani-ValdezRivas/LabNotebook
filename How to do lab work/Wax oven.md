What is a wax oven and why is it used in histology?
- A specialized laboratory incubator used to melt, store, and maintain paraffin wax at a precise, liquid state for tissue embedding and slide preparation
Chemically safe vacuum oven
- Can't keep melted wax or it will be solidified as it is pressurized, heats up as pressurized 
- Turn on two hours beforehand so it gets properly heated
	- Where do you keep wax? In the other wax oven on a glass
- Need to turn off standby:
	- Go t the touch screen and click icon w/ pointer finger
- Turn it on:
	- Set pressure less than 100, 50 would be best 
	- Set temperature to 60
	- Make sure none of the valves are clicked in the settings
	- Open the valve on the side of the oven and start it
	- Close the valves once you hear clicking
		- In the touch screen
- Need to depressurize when you go to open it
	- Open the valve on the touch screen
	- Change pressure back to ambiance (1100)
